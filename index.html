Is FixZA free for customers?

Yes. Customers can contact FixZA to request plumbing help without paying FixZA a booking fee.

Is it free for plumbers?

During the launch, we're onboarding founding plumbers for free while we test the service and build demand.

Does FixZA guarantee a plumber?

Not yet. We're building the network now, so availability depends on the plumbers who have joined in your area.
